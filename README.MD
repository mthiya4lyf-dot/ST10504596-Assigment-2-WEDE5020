## 1. Project Overview & Proposal

### Organisation Overview
* **Name:** CobraTech Solutions
* **History:** CobraTech Solutions was founded in 2024 to offer small businesses and residents of the local community high-quality, easily accessible IT support, technical repair, and graphic design services. The agency has expanded over the last two years from a local troubleshooting setup to a full-service creative and technical solution provider.
* **Mission Statement:** CobraTech Solutions provides accurate, dependable, and creative technology and branding services to empower residents and small businesses.
* **Vision Statement:** CobraTech Solutions intends to establish itself as a leading regional supplier of digital connectivity solutions, contemporary visual graphic design, and end-to-end technical support in South Africa.
* **Target Audience:** Small to medium-sized business owners, local business owners, community organizations, and individual clients seeking expert graphic design, branding materials, network setup, or hardware repairs.

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives

### Key Goals
To create an authoritative online presence, display the entire service catalog, generate service leads, and make it simple for potential customers to submit repair or design requests.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Submitting at least 20 direct service inquiries via the online form each month.
* Keeping all service pages' bounce rates below 45%.
* Guaranteeing high engagement with portfolio content by achieving an average user session duration of over two minutes.

## 3. Design & User Experience Specifications

### Colour Palette
* **Primary:** Deep Charcoal (`#1E1E1E`)
* **Secondary:** Electric Green (`#00E676`)
* **Accent / Background:** Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) and Light Slate (`#F4F6F8`)

### Typography
* **Primary Body Typeface:** Aptos / Segoe UI (Standard 11pt equivalent sizing / 1rem)
* **Headers:** Aptos Display / Bold Section Headers (`32px` bold for Heading 1, `24px` semi-bold for Heading 2)

### Layout & Architecture
* **Navigation:** Permanent top navigation bar linking all 5 core pages (`index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `enquiry.html`, `contact.html`).
* **Responsive Layout:** CSS Grid layout with single-column responsive stacking for mobile devices below `600px`.

## 4. Technical Requirements & Stack

* **Tools:** Git version management, GitHub Pages deployment, and Visual Studio Code IDE.
